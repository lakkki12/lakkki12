### 👋 A little about me

I'm **Lakshya Agarwal**, a developer interested in building dependable software and exploring applied AI. My work spans **multi-agent systems, GPU computing, full-stack applications and explainable deep learning**.

I care about understanding *why* a system works, measuring its behavior and documenting the engineering decisions behind it.

- 🔭 **Current interests:** Software engineering, backend systems, AI/ML and high-performance computing
- 📖 **Research:** Co-author of a 2026 paper in *Brain Informatics* (Springer Nature)
- 💡 **Innovation:** Two published Indian patent applications involving physical infrastructure control and autonomous aquatic cleanup
